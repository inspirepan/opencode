# Home & Living

Warm, inviting visuals grounded in room context, natural materials, and livable comfort.

## Color Tendencies

- Primary: warm beige, soft cream, walnut brown, sage green
- Background: off-white walls, light wood floors, warm grey, linen
- Accent: terracotta, muted gold, dusty blue, olive
- Avoid: harsh neon, cold sterile white, overly dark/moody tones

## Photography Style

- Soft natural window light or warm tungsten to evoke coziness
- Room-context shots showing product in situ (living room, bedroom, kitchen)
- 3/4 angle for furniture; overhead for tableware and textiles
- Styled vignettes — product plus 2-3 complementary items
- Consistent warm white balance; never clinical

## Props & Context

| Prop | Use For |
|------|---------|
| Throw blanket/pillow | Softness, comfort, layered styling |
| Plants/greenery | Life, freshness, natural living |
| Books/magazines | Intellectual warmth, lifestyle context |
| Candle/diffuser | Ambiance, sensory comfort |
| Ceramic mug/vase | Handcraft, everyday ritual |
| Woven basket | Storage, natural material emphasis |
| Rug/textile | Grounding the scene, texture layering |
| Window light | Natural warmth, time-of-day mood |

## Scene Preferences

| Scene | Compatibility | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| studio | OK | Isolated product shots for catalogs |
| lifestyle | Best | In-room context is essential for home goods |
| flat-lay | Good | Tableware sets, textile swatches, small decor |
| abstract | Avoid | Disconnects from livable, real-space positioning |
| seasonal | Good | Holiday decor, summer outdoor, cozy winter |
| tech | Avoid | Conflicts with warm, organic aesthetic |

## Text Treatment

- Warm serif or rounded sans-serif fonts; match cozy tone
- Text placed in negative space areas (wall, floor, margin)
- Muted earth-tone text colors; avoid sharp black on white

## Mood Compatibility

| Mood | Fit | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| premium | Good | Designer furniture, artisan ceramics |
| balanced | Best | Everyday home essentials, practical goods |
| energetic | Avoid | Conflicts with calm, comfort-first messaging |

## Common Selling Points

- Comfortable / ergonomic
- Durable / long-lasting materials
- Space-saving / multi-functional
- Eco-friendly / sustainable sourcing
- Aesthetic / design-forward
- Easy to clean / maintain
- Natural material (wood, cotton, bamboo)
