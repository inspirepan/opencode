# Fashion & Apparel

Texture-rich, context-driven visuals emphasizing fabric quality, fit, and style identity.

## Color Tendencies

- Primary: neutral earth tones (camel, taupe, charcoal), seasonal accent colors
- Background: off-white, warm grey, concrete, blurred street/nature
- Accent: bold seasonal pops (coral spring, burgundy autumn, navy winter)
- Avoid: overly saturated neon unless streetwear; clashing multi-color backgrounds

## Photography Style

- Natural or golden-hour lighting to render fabric texture accurately
- Full-body or 3/4 crop to show silhouette and fit
- Detail insets for stitching, weave, collar, or hardware
- Slight motion blur or wind effect to convey movement and drape
- Neutral model posing; product is hero, not the pose

## Props & Context

| Prop | Use For |
|------|---------|
| Model/mannequin | Showing fit, drape, proportion |
| Hanger + rail | Minimalist product-only presentation |
| Accessories | Completing outfit story (bag, watch, scarf) |
| Fabric swatch | Close-up texture, material callout |
| Urban backdrop | Streetwear, casual, youth positioning |
| Nature/field | Outdoor, organic, linen/cotton lines |
| Mirror | Fitting-room or editorial context |

## Scene Preferences

| Scene | Compatibility | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| studio | Good | Clean pack shots, white-background e-commerce |
| lifestyle | Best | Street, cafe, travel context tells wear story |
| flat-lay | Good | Outfit grids, accessory pairings |
| abstract | OK | Texture macro, fabric-focused brand campaigns |
| seasonal | Best | Collections tied to seasons by definition |
| tech | Avoid | No relevance to apparel |

## Text Treatment

- Sans-serif or editorial serif; match brand tier
- Text overlays on blurred background areas, not over garment detail
- Muted or tonal text colors; avoid heavy drop shadows

## Mood Compatibility

| Mood | Fit | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| premium | Good | Luxury fabrics, designer collaborations |
| balanced | Best | Everyday wear, basics, workwear |
| energetic | Good | Streetwear, athleisure, youth campaigns |

## Common Selling Points

- Breathable / moisture-wicking
- Comfortable / soft-touch fabric
- Trendy / season-relevant design
- Versatile / easy to style
- Premium fabric (cotton, silk, wool, cashmere)
- Tailored fit / flattering cut
- Sustainable / eco-friendly material
