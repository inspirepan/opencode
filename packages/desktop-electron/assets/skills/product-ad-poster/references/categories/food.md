# Food & Beverage

Warm, appetite-driven visuals emphasizing freshness, texture, and ingredient authenticity.

## Color Tendencies

- Primary: orange, golden yellow, warm brown, deep red
- Background: warm cream, light wood, terracotta, natural linen
- Accent: fresh green (herbs), white (ceramic/porcelain)
- Avoid: cold blue, neon, grey — suppress appetite

## Photography Style

- Warm directional lighting with soft shadows to enhance texture
- Top-down or 30-degree angle for plated dishes; eye-level for beverages
- Shallow depth of field on hero ingredient; keep supporting items soft
- Capture steam, drips, crumbs, or pour moments for freshness cues
- Natural light feel preferred over harsh studio flash

## Props & Context

| Prop | Use For |
|------|---------|
| Raw ingredients | Reinforcing "natural/organic" claims |
| Wooden board/table | Artisanal, handmade positioning |
| Linen napkin | Warm homestyle or bakery feel |
| Ceramic plates | Clean, modern food presentation |
| Fresh herbs | Adding color pop and freshness signal |
| Chopsticks/utensils | Cuisine-specific context |
| Spice scatter | Flavor intensity, seasoning emphasis |
| Glass bottle/jar | Beverages, sauces, preserves |

## Scene Preferences

| Scene | Compatibility | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| studio | Good | Clean pack shots, hero ingredient focus |
| lifestyle | Best | Cooking, dining, kitchen context sells story |
| flat-lay | Best | Top-down ingredient spread, recipe feel |
| abstract | Avoid | Disconnects from appetite appeal |
| seasonal | Good | Holiday feasts, summer drinks, hot-pot winter |
| tech | Avoid | No relevance to food |

## Text Treatment

- Keep text away from the hero food area; use margins or banner overlays
- Warm-toned text (cream, brown) on dark backgrounds; dark text on light
- Handwritten or serif fonts reinforce artisanal positioning

## Mood Compatibility

| Mood | Fit | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| premium | Good | Gourmet, imported, specialty items |
| balanced | Best | Everyday grocery, snacks, home cooking |
| energetic | OK | Energy drinks, spicy food, limited-time promos |

## Common Selling Points

- Fresh / farm-to-table
- Organic / natural ingredients
- Handmade / artisanal
- Authentic flavor / regional origin
- Zero additives / preservative-free
- Ready-to-eat / convenient
- Delicious / crave-worthy
