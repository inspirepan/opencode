# Kids & Baby

Bright, playful visuals with soft edges, safety cues, and cheerful warmth for parents and children.

## Color Tendencies

- Primary: soft pastel (sky blue, mint green, blush pink, lemon yellow)
- Background: clean white, light cream, soft cloud grey, pale rainbow gradient
- Accent: warm coral, sunny orange, lavender, grass green
- Avoid: dark/moody tones, harsh neon, black-heavy palettes, overly adult aesthetics

## Photography Style

- Bright, even, shadow-free lighting to convey safety and clarity
- Eye-level or slightly above for child perspective
- Rounded compositions; avoid sharp geometric framing
- Warm skin tones and natural expressions; never overly posed
- Soft focus backgrounds to keep attention on product and child interaction

## Props & Context

| Prop | Use For |
|------|---------|
| Child model | Age-appropriate fit, scale, and use context |
| Wooden toys | Natural, safe material emphasis |
| Soft blanket/mat | Comfort, nursery, tummy-time context |
| Alphabet/numbers | Educational positioning |
| Cartoon/animal motifs | Playfulness, age appeal |
| Parent hands | Safety, care, guidance context |
| Colorful blocks | Developmental play, creativity |
| Grass/park setting | Outdoor play, healthy lifestyle |

## Scene Preferences

| Scene | Compatibility | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| studio | Good | Clean product shots, white/pastel backgrounds |
| lifestyle | Best | Nursery, playroom, park — real-use context |
| flat-lay | Good | Gift sets, outfit grids, toy collections |
| abstract | OK | Soft shapes, cloud/star patterns as backgrounds |
| seasonal | Good | Back-to-school, holiday gifts, summer outdoor |
| tech | Avoid | Conflicts with warm, organic, child-safe tone |

## Text Treatment

- Rounded sans-serif or friendly handwritten fonts
- Soft pastel or warm-colored text; avoid harsh black on white
- Large, readable type; parents scan quickly

## Mood Compatibility

| Mood | Fit | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| premium | OK | Organic baby lines, designer children's brands |
| balanced | Best | Everyday kids' essentials, practical purchases |
| energetic | Good | Toys, outdoor play gear, party themes |

## Common Selling Points

- Safe / non-toxic / BPA-free
- Educational / developmental
- Fun / engaging for kids
- Durable / withstands rough play
- Age-appropriate / certified
- Soft / skin-friendly materials
- Parent-approved / easy to clean
