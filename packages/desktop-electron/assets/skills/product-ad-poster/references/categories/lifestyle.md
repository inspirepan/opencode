# Lifestyle & Accessories

Soft, editorial-toned visuals with curated context, emphasizing design taste and daily ritual.

## Color Tendencies

- Primary: warm taupe, soft white, muted terracotta, sand
- Background: light marble, linen cloth, wooden desk, neutral paper
- Accent: brass/gold hardware, forest green, dusty rose
- Avoid: harsh saturated colors, busy patterns, cold industrial grey

## Photography Style

- Soft diffused natural light; editorial window-lit feel
- Intentional negative space for breathing room and text placement
- Detail shots for material, hardware, stitching, texture
- Slight depth of field to create layered, curated compositions
- Warm-neutral color grading; cohesive tonal palette per frame

## Props & Context

| Prop | Use For |
|------|---------|
| Desk/table surface | Everyday carry, stationery, accessories |
| Coffee cup | Morning ritual, lifestyle context |
| Notebook/pen | Creative, professional positioning |
| Leather goods | Quality, craftsmanship association |
| Dried flowers | Subtle organic warmth without clutter |
| Travel pouch/bag | Portability, on-the-go use case |
| Gift box/wrapping | Gifting occasion, unboxing moment |
| Sunlight/shadow | Natural, editorial mood layer |

## Scene Preferences

| Scene | Compatibility | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| studio | Good | Clean hero shots with warm backdrop |
| lifestyle | Best | Desk, cafe, travel, daily-use context |
| flat-lay | Best | Curated grid, everyday carry, collection layout |
| abstract | OK | Minimal texture backgrounds for brand campaigns |
| seasonal | Good | Holiday gifting, back-to-school, travel season |
| tech | Avoid | Too cold for lifestyle warmth |

## Text Treatment

- Elegant thin sans-serif or modern serif; editorial magazine feel
- Generous whitespace; text as a design element, not overlay
- Muted tonal colors (warm grey, taupe) for copy; gold for accents

## Mood Compatibility

| Mood | Fit | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| premium | Best | Leather goods, designer accessories, gift sets |
| balanced | Best | Everyday accessories, stationery, organizers |
| energetic | OK | Festival merch, limited drops, youth accessories |

## Common Selling Points

- Premium quality / craftsmanship
- Thoughtful design / aesthetic detail
- Portable / compact
- Creative / inspirational
- Perfect gift / gift-ready packaging
- Versatile / everyday essential
- Handcrafted / limited edition
