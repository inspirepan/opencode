# Float

Product appears floating with shadow or glow underneath, surrounded by orbiting decorative elements.

## Composition

- Product zone: 40%, centered, elevated from visual ground plane
- Text zone: 25-30%, below product or overlaid at top
- Decorative zone: ring around product (icons, ingredients, features, particles)

## Spatial Guide

```
+----------------------------+
|       [ headline ]         |
|    *        *        *     |
|  *    +-----------+    *   |
|  *    |  PRODUCT  |    *   |
|  *    | (floating)|    *   |
|    *  +-----------+  *     |
|       ~~~shadow~~~         |
|        [ price ]           |
+----------------------------+
```

## Best For

- Lightweight products (earbuds, cosmetics, supplements)
- Tech gadgets and accessories
- Products with multiple feature callouts
- Creative or whimsical brand identity

## Scene Compatibility

| Scene | Fit | Notes |
|-------|-----|-------|
| studio | Best | Clean void maximizes the floating illusion |
| lifestyle | Avoid | Real-world context breaks the floating effect |
| flat-lay | Avoid | Top-down perspective contradicts the float illusion |
| abstract | Best | Gradients and particles enhance the ethereal feel |
| seasonal | OK | Subtle seasonal particles can orbit with the product |
| tech | Best | Glow effects and dark backgrounds amplify tech appeal |

## Prompt Notes

- Render a soft elliptical shadow or luminous glow directly beneath the product to sell the float
- Orbiting elements should be small (15-20% of product size) and evenly spaced to avoid clutter
- Use a gradient or solid dark/light background; avoid detailed scene backdrops that undermine the float
